Chapter 02About me

自己紹介

I'm a UX designer from Mumbai, currently pursuing an M.Des in Intelligent User Experience Design at Dhirubhai Ambani University.

I focus on modern, scalable, user-centered digital products. My work starts with research, moves through structured systems and information architecture, and ends in interfaces that feel clear and intentional.

Before design, I spent two years in counseling and customer service. Sitting across from frustrated users every day taught me how much a confusing flow costs a real person. That is still the lens I design with.
